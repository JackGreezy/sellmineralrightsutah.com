# Sell Mineral Rights Utah — Site-Specific Rules

This project has its own Utah mineral-evidence voice and its own EOS-derived visual system. Never import a reusable mineral-rights voice pack or reuse another site's hero, FAQ, related rail, article ending, CTA, or decorative language.

## Voice lock

Write as a Utah mineral evidence console. Use legal descriptions, township-range-section calls, private and public land boundaries, SITLA checkerboard context, Uinta waxy-crude logistics, Paradox Basin evidence, paid decimals, heat-and-haul deductions, and a written decision file. Never use first-person singular business narration, guaranteed value, instant-cash language, or a highest-offer claim.

## Visual lock

Preserve EOS's real IBM Plex Sans typography, deep graphite surfaces, orange action color, compact radii, satellite-data card rhythm, analytical tabs, layered dark bands, and measured carousel controls. Slug pages use one wide hero image, broad readable evidence content, a Utah data-check FAQ, connected-layer related links, and an orange decision-file CTA. Never insert another site's generic rounded card stack.

## Release blockers

- The street address may appear only inside a Google Maps embed query.
- No footer thumbnails, blank images, tiny images, floating media glitches, emoji, or Unicode-arrow decoration.
- Every slug has exactly one image: its hero image.
- No vertical one-character text, squeezed desktop columns, invisible copy, weak contrast, clipped sections, or overlapping header elements.
- Desktop header includes real routes, phone, and a clear contact CTA. Dropdowns work by hover and focus without a pointer gap; the mobile menu and every carousel control work.
- Contact forms contain exactly Name, Email, Phone, and Message.
- Every footer contains a Google Maps embed.
- The homepage hero contains an SEO-driven H1.
- Every page retains the EOS-derived fonts and theme.

Completion requires both machine gates, full desktop/mobile screenshot review, production deployment, and live alias verification.
